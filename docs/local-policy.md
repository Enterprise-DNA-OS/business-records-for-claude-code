# Local operating policy

These are example business controls, not statutory deadlines.

- Every active record needs a named owner, a class, a retained original and an explicit retention date.
- Unknown source classes stay unclassified. No default period is silently invented.
- An operator enters the tax-period end when choosing nz-tax. The seven-year floor is checked separately from the recorded retention date.
- Review intervals and personal-information purpose decisions belong to the business. The software supplies no legal default interval.
- A reviewed record names a current revision and original version, with a future next-review date and evidence reference.
- A hold blocks any new disposal proposal. A release needs a written instruction reference.
- Archive means inactive, with all originals and holds retained. It is not destruction.
- Original files are limited to 50 MB each. The program does not execute or render their contents. Scan them for malware before opening them with another application.
- The storage-location, access-review and restore-test controls are manually evidenced. Their names do not imply the software performed those checks.
- Treat generated reports as private business records. Protect their output directory and apply your own retention rules.
